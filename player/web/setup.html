    Tests

    Screen

    Shows a test picture on the player for 5 seconds. Check that the border reaches every edge and the text is the right way up.

    DAC

    Pauses playback and plays a quiet tone: a low one in the left ear, then a higher one in the right. Plug in headphones or speakers first.

    Buttons and knob

    Press each button and turn the knob; each one lights up here when the player sees it. Clockwise should read “clockwise”.
